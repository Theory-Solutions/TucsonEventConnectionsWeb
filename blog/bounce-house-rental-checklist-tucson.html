Bounce House Rental Checklist: What to Ask Before You Book in Tucson

Updated July 2026 · Tucson Event Connections

Bounce house rentals are usually a fun, low-stress part of party planning — but they're also inflatable equipment set up in your backyard or a public park with kids climbing on it. A few quick questions before you book can save you from a cancelled party, a surprise fee, or worse, an unsafe setup.

Here's exactly what to ask any Tucson-area rental company before you hand over a deposit.

Insurance & Licensing

  • Are you licensed and insured, and can you provide proof of liability coverage?
  • If you're booking for a public park (City of Tucson, Pima County, or Marana), does the company have the specific permit/license required for that park district?

Setup Requirements

  • What surface does the unit need (grass, concrete, dirt) and do you have that at your location?
  • Is a generator required, and is that included in the quote or billed separately?
  • How much space (including clearance around the unit) does the specific inflatable need?
  • Is there a separate park usage fee if you're booking for a public park?

Weather & Cancellation Policy

  • What's the policy for wind or rain — Tucson's monsoon season (July–September) can bring fast-moving storms with little warning
  • Who's responsible for calling off the rental if weather turns — the company, or do you need to call them?
  • Is there a cancellation fee, and how close to the event date can you cancel without one?

What's Actually Included

  • Does the quoted price include delivery, setup, and pickup, or are those extra?
  • Is there a delivery fee based on your zip code — many companies offer free delivery within a set radius, then charge beyond it
  • How many hours does the rental cover, and what's the fee for extending it same-day?
  • Is the unit cleaned/sanitized between rentals, and when was it last inspected?

Supervision & Safety

  • What's the max weight/number of kids and the recommended age range for the specific unit?
  • Does the company provide any setup safety briefing, or is supervision entirely on you?
  • Are there separate units recommended for mixed age groups (younger kids vs. older kids/obstacle courses)?

Tucson-specific tip: If your event falls between July and September, ask the vendor directly how they handle monsoon weather. Fast-moving storms are common, and a vendor with a clear, calm policy for cancelling or rescheduling is a good sign of how they'll handle other problems too.

Red Flags to Watch For

  • A company that can't produce proof of insurance when asked directly
  • Pricing that seems noticeably below the typical Tucson range with no clear explanation
  • No clear answer on what happens if it rains or the wind picks up
  • Reluctance to confirm delivery/setup times in writing
