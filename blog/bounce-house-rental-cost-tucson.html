How Much Does It Cost to Rent a Bounce House in Tucson? (2026 Price Guide)

Updated July 2026 · Tucson Event Connections

If you're planning a birthday party, school event, or backyard get-together in Tucson, a bounce house is usually one of the first things parents look into — and one of the first questions is always the same: how much is this actually going to cost?

Here's a straightforward breakdown of what to expect in the Tucson market in 2026, based on typical rates from local inflatable rental companies.

Typical Bounce House Rental Prices in Tucson

Across Tucson-area rental companies, standard residential bounce house and inflatable rentals typically run $150–$450 per day, with delivery and setup usually included in that range. Where you land in that range depends mostly on the type of unit and the extras you add on.

Inflatable TypeWhat It's Good ForTypical Price Range
Classic Bounce HouseSimple castle/bouncer, up to ~10 kids$150–$225 / day
Combo Unit (Bounce + Slide)The most popular option for birthday parties$225–$325 / day
Water SlideSpring/summer parties and backyard events$225–$375 / day
Obstacle CourseOlder kids, adults, school & corporate events$300–$450 / day

These are day-rate ranges for standard residential delivery. Larger commercial-grade units, multi-lane obstacle courses, and specialty/licensed-character themes tend to sit at the top of the range or slightly above it.

What Actually Changes the Price

  • Unit size and type — a simple bounce house costs less than a combo unit with a slide, and multi-lane obstacle courses cost more than either
  • Delivery distance — many Tucson companies include free delivery within a set radius of their warehouse, then charge a fee beyond that — worth confirming your zip code up front
  • Park or venue rentals — public park bookings often add a generator fee (since most parks don't have accessible power) plus a separate park permit/usage fee
  • Rental duration — most quotes are for a standard several-hour block; all-day or multi-day rentals typically cost more
  • Add-ons — tables, chairs, concession machines (popcorn, snow cones, cotton candy), and generators are usually priced separately from the inflatable itself

Good to know: Renting for a public park in Tucson? Most parks require the rental company to be licensed and insured for that specific park district (City of Tucson, Pima County, and Marana each have their own requirements), and you'll likely need a generator since most park sites don't have outlets nearby.

How to Avoid Surprise Fees

The quoted "day rate" isn't always the full price you'll pay at checkout. Before booking, it's worth asking every vendor you're considering for a full breakdown that includes delivery, setup/breakdown, any generator or park fees, and the exact rental window (many quotes are based on a set number of hours, not a full day).
