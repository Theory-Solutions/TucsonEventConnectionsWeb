Catering Checklist: What to Ask Before You Book a Tucson Caterer

Updated July 2026 · Tucson Event Connections

Catering is one of the biggest-ticket vendor decisions for almost any event — and one of the easiest places to get an unpleasant surprise if you don't ask the right questions up front. Here's exactly what to cover with any Tucson caterer before you sign anything or hand over a deposit.

Pricing & Deposits

  • Is the quote per-person, or does it change based on final guest count?
  • What's the deposit amount, and is it refundable if plans change?
  • Is gratuity/service charge included in the quote, or added separately at the end?
  • What's the cutoff date for your final headcount, and what happens if it changes after that?

What's Actually Included

  • Does the price include setup, serving staff, and cleanup — or is it drop-off only?
  • Are plates, cups, napkins, and utensils included, or do you need to source those separately?
  • Are tables, linens, and chafing equipment included, or billed as rentals?
  • Is delivery included, or is there a separate delivery fee based on distance?

Menu & Dietary Accommodations

  • Can the menu accommodate vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, or allergy-specific requests?
  • Is there a tasting available before you commit, and is it free or paid?
  • How far in advance do menu changes need to be finalized?

Logistics & Timing

  • How far in advance do they need to book for your event date — especially during Tucson's peak wedding season (October–April)?
  • What's their cancellation and rescheduling policy?
  • For outdoor Tucson events: how do they handle food safety in the heat — cold-holding equipment, shaded service areas, timing service after sunset?

Tucson-specific tip: If your event is outdoors during the hotter months, ask directly how the caterer plans to keep food safe — mayo-based dishes and anything needing refrigeration are a real risk sitting out in Tucson heat. A caterer with a clear, specific answer here is usually a good sign across the board.

Red Flags to Watch For

  • Vague answers about what's included in the quoted price
  • No clear tasting or menu-sample process before you commit
  • Reluctance to put deposit and cancellation terms in writing
  • No specific plan for outdoor food safety during Tucson's hot months
